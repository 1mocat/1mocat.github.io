Hello I'm

Chengquan Guo (郭城权)

I am a first year PhD student in the Secure Learning Lab at the University of Chicago advised by Prof. Bo Li and Prof. Ce Zhang. My research interests lie in Trustworthy AI, with a focus on building reliable, safe, and robust AI systems for real-world applications. I am a research intern at Microsoft Research in Summer 2026.

Research

Much of my work follows a single through-line on code-agent safety: first measure the risk, then automate the attack, then turn the attack into a defense.

01 · MEASURE RedCode Benchmark the risk A benchmark exposing how code agents execute and generate risky code. → 02 · ATTACK RedCodeAgent Automate red teaming An adaptive agent that discovers those failures on its own, at scale. → 03 · DEFEND BlueCodeAgent Attacks become defenses Automated red teaming feeds a blue-teaming agent that guards CodeGen AI.

    "*" stands for equal contribution.
